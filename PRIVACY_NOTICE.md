# Privacy notice draft

This local software demo processes email addresses, password hashes, optional phone numbers, vehicle plate text, booking windows, and parking events to provide reservations and operations. The original plate is retained alongside a normalized comparison copy. Driver endpoints scope bookings to the authenticated driver. The attendant plate lookup returns a masked plate, and the attendant vehicle listing masks plates. This does not establish that every staff screen, log, export, or operational process masks personal data; use synthetic data only until a full privacy review is complete.

Demo data is synthetic. Payment is a simulator in TEST MODE; raw card data is neither requested nor stored. The system does not include cameras, ANPR, sensors, barriers, live payments, or automated fines, bans, or blacklisting. A flag only requests human review.

Retention periods, deletion-on-request, consent wording, provider data handling, DPDP Act obligations, and a production privacy assessment require operator and legal review. This draft is not a compliance claim.
