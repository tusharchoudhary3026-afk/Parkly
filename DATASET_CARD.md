# Dataset card

The `python -m app.seed` command uses fixed seed `20261101` to create 3 fictional lots, 201 fictional slots, 120 synthetic driver accounts/vehicles, and 30 days of synthetic completed bookings with weekday, weekend, and peak-hour variation. It also creates 12 synthetic review-signal examples and 12 legitimate-lookalike examples with explicit `synthetic_label` values. Every seeded account, lot, plate, booking, snapshot, and review example is synthetic. The records are generated assumptions and are not representative of a city, real people, or real demand.

These labels were authored with the synthetic data generator and have not been independently assigned or double-labelled; they are demonstration examples, not a validated rule-quality dataset. No real-world accuracy or fairness result is claimed. The final-current-source AT-09 synthetic evaluation held out the last seven days and reported baseline MAE 0.0287 with n=504 snapshots; this measures only the deterministic synthetic fixture and is not a forecast accuracy claim for real lots.

The location-discovery extension adds shared reference data for **36 Indian states and union territories, 70 cities, 19 Gwalior areas, and 38 fictional venues**. The non-Gwalior city rows are starter reference entries; Gwalior localities are name-only entries and have not been independently verified. Venues are explicitly labelled synthetic. Three existing synthetic lots may be linked to the seeded Gwalior venues; this does not make other venues bookable. Seed IDs are deterministic and the location seed is designed to be idempotent. This is an `EXTENSION` beyond the numbered PDF requirements.
